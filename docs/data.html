Data files

Aggregates only: no model weights, no model outputs from the private benchmark, no benchmark items. The edit directions are published as vectors, together with the formula that applies them.

Part 1: the 0.8B

FileWhat
bytes.jsonThe byte comparison: tensors that differ, counts, hashes, the rank-one fit, the dose per column, the rebuild.
directions_0p8b.jsonThe two recovered directions u (1,024 numbers each) and the 64 edited columns per layer. Derived from MIT CSAIL's release (CC BY 4.0).
released_regrade.jsonThe released MATH-500 texts re-graded on CPU, and the authors' flags re-summed.
settings.jsonThe three sampling settings, seeds, engine and prompt.
math500_settings.csvSix runs: accuracy, standard error, share at the token cap, tokens, time on one RTX 3090.
math500_paired.csvPaired differences, edited − base, per setting.
math500_same100.csv, math500_same100_pairs.csvAll six arms on the same 100 problems, and the cross-setting comparisons.
order100.txtThe 100 MATH-500 problem ids used for C1 and C2.
mit_reported_ten_benchmarks.csvThe authors' ten benchmarks, read from their logs (not re-run).
costs.jsonPerplexity, tool calling, short and long answers, and the agent task (aggregates).

Part 2: our 4B

FileWhat
base_4b_math_practice.jsonDoes the 4B loop? 128 practice answers under the post's settings.
contrast_set_and_survey.jsonCounts of the contrast set, the split, the direction survey, and summary figures quoted on the page.
teacher_forced_screen.csvThe four families at doses 1–6 and their controls.
direction_4b_F1.jsonThe chosen edit: per layer u (2,560 numbers) and 165 columns, plus the random control.
dose_screen.csvThe 24-item screen: one run per dose and arm.
full_benchmark_runs.csvEvery full run: citations held (all, practice, held-out), misses by kind, valid answers, whole-benchmark pass.
bootstrap.csvPooled differences and 95% intervals: the planned test, the extension, and the extension alone.
perplexity_by_dose.csv, perplexity_note.jsonPerplexity of every dose, edit and control.
cost.jsonGPU jobs and time.
